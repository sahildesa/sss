Mold Creation

With the precision mold ready, the next phase brings the coin to life — the infusion of molten Cadmium metal. Metal is heated to extreme temperatures, turning it into a glowing molten form.

The molten metal is carefully poured into the precision mold, flowing into every fine detail. This phase lays the structural foundation of the coin, turning concept into tangible reality.

The process captures the 3D design and textures from the mold with accuracy and depth.

The Craft Behind CoinZee: From Metal to Masterpiece.

Pouring the Metal

Cadmium metal is heated to high temperatures.
Glowing molten metal is poured into precision molds,
as it flows into intricate design details with accuracy.

Metal Embryo Formation

Molten metal cools and solidifies.
This forms the coin's 3D base — the “metal embryo”.
Captures all textures and depth from the mold.

Polishing & Plating

Raw coin is polished to enhance surface finish.
Coating is applied (gold, silver, etc.) for shine and durability,
and preps the coin for final coloring and assembly.
