Privacy Policy – SMS Communications

Last updated: August 10, 2025

How We Collect and Use Consumer Data for SMS

At Southern Sourcing Supply LLC, we take your privacy seriously. When you opt in to receive SMS communications from us, here is how we handle your personal information:

  • What We Collect: We collect your phone number, and where applicable, your name and email address—only when you provide this information through our website, forms, or SMS opt-in mechanisms.
  • How We Collect It: We may collect your data through our online forms, in-person interactions, or keyword opt-in processes (e.g., texting SUBSCRIBE).
  • Purposes of Use: Your data is used exclusively to deliver SMS messages such as order updates, service alerts, reminders, and marketing messages—only if you have opted in to receive them.
  • Non-Sharing Policy: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for marketing purposes. Your information is used solely for communication between you and Southern Sourcing Supply LLC.
  • Security & Retention: We protect your data using secure storage and encryption. We retain your consent data only as long as you are subscribed and delete or anonymize it upon request or account closure.

How Consumers Can Contact Us Regarding SMS Communications

Your preferences and concerns matter to us. Please use any of the following methods to reach out with questions or requests about our SMS program:

  • Text HELP: Reply HELP to any SMS you receive to get instant assistance.
  • Email Support: Contact our team at support@southernsourcingsupply.com , and we’ll respond within 24–48 hours.
  • Opt-Out Notice: You can opt out by replying STOP to any message at any time. We’ll confirm your unsubscribe request via SMS.
  • Alternatively, visit our contact page or account settings on our website to manage your messaging preferences.
